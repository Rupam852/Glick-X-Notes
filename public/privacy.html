Privacy Policy for Glick X Notes

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Glick X Notes (https://glick-x-notes.vercel.app). We are dedicated to providing a private, secure, and modern digital workspace. Your privacy is paramount to us, and we are committed to safeguarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Glick X Notes collects only the minimal data essential to provide our note-taking services:

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in using Google OAuth or Email/Password, we receive your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID) provided by Firebase Authentication.
  • User-Generated Content: Notes, text content, titles, custom tags, folder organization categories, and multi-media attachments created or uploaded within your personal workspace.
  • Local Device Preferences: Theme settings (Dark/Light mode) and local offline cache data stored in browser LocalStorage/IndexedDB.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and provide you secure access to your personal notebooks.
  • To synchronize your notes across your devices in real-time.
  • To enable offline editing and caching on your device.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or commercialize your personal data or notes to any third parties.
  • We do NOT use your private notes to train machine learning models.

3. Zero-Trust Data Isolation & Storage Security

Your notes and attachments are stored on Google Cloud Firestore database infrastructure. We enforce strict database-level Security Rules:

  • Every read, write, update, and delete request is cryptographically tied to your unique user ID (UID).
  • No other user can read, query, or modify your personal notes.
  • All data transmissions between your browser and our cloud servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL).

4. Google API & OAuth User Data Policy

Glick X Notes strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. When you use Google Sign-In, we access only your basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture) to establish your account.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain full control and 100% intellectual property ownership over your content:

  • You can edit or permanently delete any note or attachment at any time from within the application.
  • When you delete a note or attachment, it is immediately and permanently purged from Cloud Firestore.
  • To request full account deletion, you may email us directly at the contact address below.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Glick X Notes does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. We use standard browser LocalStorage and IndexedDB strictly for storing authentication tokens and offline cached notes.
